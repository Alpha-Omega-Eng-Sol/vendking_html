How to Reach Us

By Car/Taxi

From Kathmandu Ring Road, take the route towards Kirtipur. Follow signs to Tyanglaphat Chowk. Our office is located at Chadani Chowk, easily accessible from the main road.

Parking available on-site

By Public Transport

Take a bus from Ratna Park or New Bus Park to Kirtipur. Get off at Tyanglaphat Chowk and walk 2-3 minutes to reach Chadani Chowk.

Local buses run frequently throughout the day
