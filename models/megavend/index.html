megaVend

Turning empties into a cleaner Nepal

megaVend is a reverse vending machine: instead of selling products, it takes back used bottles and cans so they can be recycled. It isn't manufactured yet. We're developing it now, and like the rest of our machines it will connect to the Vending King Master App.

  • Collects bottles and cans for recycling
  • Planned for campuses, malls and public spaces
  • Will be managed from the Master App
