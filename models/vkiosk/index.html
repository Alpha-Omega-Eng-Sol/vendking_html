In use at cafés & restaurants

VKiosk

Self-ordering, built for 24/7 use

VKiosk is designed and manufactured by Vending King in Nepal. Cafés and restaurants use it for food ordering: customers browse the menu, pay at the kiosk, and the bill prints automatically. That means shorter queues and fewer order mistakes at busy times.

  • Bright 21.5" Full HD touch screen, easy to read even in sunlit spaces
  • Built-in 80 mm thermal printer with auto-cutter for bills
  • Windows 10 PC inside, with LAN, USB and HDMI for your setup
  • Sturdy commercial-grade stand made for all-day use

Hardware by Vending King, software by IMS Software. The ordering and billing software currently running on VKiosk is provided by our partner, IMS Software.

How it works

From order to bill in four steps

  1. 01

    Browse & order

    Customers pick items from the menu on the 21.5" touch screen.

  2. 02

    Pay

    They pay right at the kiosk before the order is placed.

  3. 03

    Get the bill

    The built-in thermal printer prints the bill straight away.

  4. 04

    Kitchen gets it

    The order goes through to your counter or kitchen.
